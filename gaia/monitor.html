The governing systems

Feedback
GaiaCoordinator

Advances time and records outcomes. No automatic rescue or reseeding.

MetisActive

Observes resource strain and the living web, then moderates births and asks governance to conserve more aggressively.

ChlorisPlant layer

Grows vegetation from seeds, soil, water, and season. Flowers sustain pollinators; plant growth creates forage; grazers consume vegetation.

PoseidonActive

Transfers water from a finite reservoir. Pumping produces pollution.

DemeterActive

Does not assign roles. Each inhabitant adapts cultivation and foraging effort from inherited aptitude, learned payoff, and current resources.

ThorActive

Produces food using water and soil fertility, with a pollution cost.

GovernanceActive

Sets conservation and cleanup effort. Cleanup uses food and uncommitted effort.

InhabitantsEvolving

Consume, reproduce, inherit traits, adapt their effort, and die. Nature replenishes a separate wild-food stock.

About this experiment

These are abstract ecological rules, not calibrated predictions. A finite run can show local steadiness, stress, or extinction. It cannot establish eternal balance or endless collapse.

Rain follows the same sequence for the same seed across regulation modes. Births and deaths may diverge.
